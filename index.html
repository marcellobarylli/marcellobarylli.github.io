GraphJane

Obsidian for DJs. Every song is a node and every transition you have found to work is a directed edge, storing where to mix out, where to mix in and the tempo of both tracks. A DJ set is a walk through that graph.

Most DJs keep this knowledge in their heads or in scattered notes. GraphJane turns it into a map you can build on: log a new mix in one card, see every way out of the song that is playing, plan sets with their tempo curve, and follow a bar clock that counts down to the next mix during the gig. It installs on a phone, works offline, and keeps separate crates per genre, like Obsidian vaults.
